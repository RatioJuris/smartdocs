Ratio Juris Smart Scanner is a modern desktop document scanning application designed to make everyday document digitisation faster and easier. It helps users turn photographed or scanned documents into clean, properly aligned digital documents.

Whether you are a **lawyer, legal researcher, student, office professional, researcher, teacher, or anyone working with paper documents**, Smart Scanner provides a simple way to prepare documents for digital use.

## ✨ Features

### Smart Document Scanning

Capture and process documents without complicated workflows. Smart Scanner helps prepare your documents for clear digital viewing and storage.

### Automatic Document Alignment

Documents photographed at an angle can be automatically detected and aligned, helping produce a clean, straight document.

### Perspective Correction

Correct documents captured from different angles so that the final result looks more like a properly scanned page.

### Image Enhancement

Improve the appearance and readability of documents by processing the captured image for cleaner digital copies.

### Easy Document Cropping

Adjust document boundaries and crop unwanted areas for a cleaner final result.

### PDF Support

Prepare documents for convenient digital workflows, including PDF-based use.

### Clean Desktop Experience

A focused Windows application designed around document scanning and preparation without unnecessary complexity.

## Why Ratio Juris Smart Scanner?

Traditional document scanning can involve several separate steps: capturing an image, correcting its angle, cropping the page, improving readability and preparing the final document.

Smart Scanner brings these everyday document-preparation tasks together in one application.

**Capture → Align → Correct → Enhance → Save**

The goal is simple: **make paper documents easier to use digitally.**
